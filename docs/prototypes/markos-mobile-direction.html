Candidate 01 · Sunlit Social Studio

A campaign, within reach.

One workspace across three platforms. A focused phone experience, with room to expand on a larger screen.

01 / NAVIGATION

Five familiar destinations.

Overview, Campaigns, Create, Calendar and Insights. Business Profile and Settings live in the workspace menu on phones and the sidebar on desktop.

02 / TRY THE JOURNEY

Brief → plan → review.

Click New campaign. Edit the sample context, add or remove file names, then continue to Plan. Create campaign shows a simulated running state.

No files are uploaded. No AI request, account change or publication is made.

03 / RECOVERY

Useful states, deliberate actions.

Use Sample state to inspect empty, loading, error and blocked screens. Previewing or marking a draft ready does not schedule it.
