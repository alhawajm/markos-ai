## English

Short description: Plan campaigns, create posts and Reels, and organize your Instagram marketing.

Description:

Turn your business knowledge into a clearer Instagram plan with MARKOS.

Build your business profile, plan a campaign, and create content in one workspace. Add up to five campaign references, including proposals, event details and visual inspiration, so your brief carries the context that matters.

- Draft captions and content with the MARKOS assistant.
- Create posts, carousels, Stories and Reels, then review the full preview.
- Turn your own artwork into Motion Reels with exact English or Arabic text cards.
- Organize campaigns and schedule reviewed content for a connected, eligible Instagram professional account.
- Review available Instagram insights and download or request monthly reports.
- Work in English or Arabic, manage team access, and keep your web and mobile workspaces together.

You stay in control of approval and scheduling. Instagram features depend on your account permissions and supported media. AI output needs your review. MARKOS is an independent product and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Instagram or Meta.
